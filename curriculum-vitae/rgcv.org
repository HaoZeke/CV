**** Fonts :ignoreheading:ignore:noexport:
***** Kerning
Interword spacing is better preserved:
#+LATEX_HEADER: \microtypecontext{spacing=nonfrench}
With more effects from microtype with kerning.
#+LATEX_HEADER: \SetExtraKerning[unit=space]
#+LATEX_HEADER: {encoding={*}, family={bch}, series={*}, size={footnotesize,small,normalsize}}
en-dashes with more space around it
#+LATEX_HEADER: {\textendash={400,400},
#+LATEX_HEADER: "28={ ,150},           % Left bracket, add space from right
#+LATEX_HEADER: "29={150, },           % Right bracket, add space from left
#+LATEX_HEADER: \textquotedblleft={ ,150},        % Left quotation mark, space from right
#+LATEX_HEADER: \textquotedblright={150, }}       % Right quotation mark, space from left
Better small caps with tracking works too
#+LATEX_HEADER: \SetTracking{encoding={*}, shape=sc}{40}
This enables better optical views.
#+LATEX_HEADER: \SetProtrusion{encoding={*},family={bch},series={*},size={6,7}}
