* Design Note :noexport:

Grant-review CVs are not compressed full CVs. The source scan used the local
IRF proposal workspace plus current funder/reviewer guidance: NIH defines a
biosketch as evidence that project staff have the skills, knowledge, and
resources for the proposed work; ERC material foregrounds independence, track
record, leadership promise, and host conditions; NSF biosketch practice keeps
the document product- and contribution-centred; NN/g scan-path work supports
front-loading high-value claims in left-anchored, strongly labelled blocks.

Design consequences for this variant:
- Page 1 makes the ESCAPE fit explicit: GP saddle search, MLIP descriptors,
  eOn/aKMC, amsel superbasins, rate corrections, excited states, and open
  infrastructure.
- Page 2 proves the fit with selected papers, grants, software, and talks.
- Page 3 gives career context, teaching/mentorship, service, review load, and
  affiliations, while keeping the David Deutsch quote from the full CV.
- The file remains orgmode-first and sources claims from
  =curriculum-vitae/literate_cv.org=, =twoPage/literate_twopage.org=, and the
  local IRF proposal at =../uniApplications/rannis_2027_project=.
